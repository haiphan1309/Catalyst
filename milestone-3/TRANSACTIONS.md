The ten pools they work on were prepared by our team before the test: see [Pools prepared before the test](#pools-prepared-before-the-test). ## What holds for all 75 transactions

- Each sell order, bid, buy, sale to bids, update and cancel carries the app's message under Cardano metadata label 674: *Bond DEX: Create Ask Order*, *Bond DEX: Buy Ask Orders*, *Bond DEX: Update Ask Order*, *Bond DEX: Cancel Ask Order*, *Bond DEX: Create Bid Order*, *Bond DEX: Sell To Bid Orders*, *Bond DEX: Update Bid Order* or *Bond DEX: Cancel Bid Order*. The four redeems carry *Fixed-Rate Lending: Redeem Pool*.
- Every sell order locks its PT, with 2 ADA, at the order contract. As in Milestone 2, the address starts with `addr_test1zq4qng…` and ends with the seller's own staking credential: open [wallet 1's orders](https://preview.cardanoscan.io/address/addr_test1zq4qngypk7czqwtf23jpe4ucukrtm72z4jjrv9rty0tewucudwk64wkcpnsyu9mcg2nu3kxkuyspwrh5x5ghj0u94ucs7uvhup) or [wallet 2's orders](https://preview.cardanoscan.io/address/addr_test1zq4qngypk7czqwtf23jpe4ucukrtm72z4jjrv9rty0tewu662s4ndurtq2k8pf8laeqzpgnn9rcgrp8wpae58wnsa3dq4k6kna) on Cardanoscan.
- Every bid locks its money, with 2 ADA, at the bid contract. A bid in ADA locks the amount plus 2 ADA (BID-01: 202 ADA); a bid in a token locks the token and 2 ADA. The address starts with `addr_test1zzzwjkp…` and ends with the bidder's staking credential: open [wallet 1's bids](https://preview.cardanoscan.io/address/addr_test1zzzwjkptwlmu90fzgjht74hqacsjh4w9xu4vwg6la7qggzgudwk64wkcpnsyu9mcg2nu3kxkuyspwrh5x5ghj0u94ucsgznmdg) or [wallet 2's bids](https://preview.cardanoscan.io/address/addr_test1zzzwjkptwlmu90fzgjht74hqacsjh4w9xu4vwg6la7qggz262s4ndurtq2k8pf8laeqzpgnn9rcgrp8wpae58wnsa3dqrg96z5) on Cardanoscan.
- Every buy, sale to bids, update and cancel spends those orders at the contract, and the chain accepted every one of these contract runs (`valid_contract = true`). The cancels and redeems on matured pools also ran with `valid_contract = true`.
- In every trade paid in USDC, DJED or SNEK, the protocol fee address receives the buyer's 0.2% and the seller's 0.1% of the price, each rounded to the smallest unit. In the ADA trades the fee output holds at least the minimum ADA of a Cardano output: 1.01716 ADA in BUY-06, BUY-07, SELL-01 and SELL-05.

## How to check a transaction

1. Open the link, or paste the hash at https://preview.cardanoscan.io.
2. The **Metadata** tab names the action. *Ask order* means sell order, *Bid order* means bid.
3. What each action shows on chain:
   - **Sell order:** the PT and 2 ADA move from the seller's wallet to the order contract.
   - **Buy:** the orders are spent; the PT goes to the buyer and the sellers are paid in the same transaction, with the fees sent to the protocol fee address. After a partial buy, the rest of the order goes back to the order contract with the same asking yield.
   - **Bid:** the bidder's money and 2 ADA move to the bid contract.
   - **Sell to bids:** the bids are spent; the seller's PT goes to the bidders and the seller is paid in the same transaction. A bid that was only partly filled goes back to the bid contract with what is left; a bid that was filled out is gone and its change returns to the bidder. If what would be left on a bid is below the pool's minimum, the bid closes in the same transaction and the rest returns to the bidder (SELL-01: BID-02 closed with 88 ADA left; SELL-03: BID-05).
   - **Update:** the order or bid is spent and replaced, in the same transaction, by one with the new amount or yield.
   - **Cancel:** the order or bid is spent and its PT or money, and the ADA it held, go back to its owner.
   - **Redeem:** the PT of a matured pool is exchanged for the pool's token. A sell order still listed is spent in the same transaction and its PT is redeemed too (MAT-05). In MAT-02 the pool paid its ADA as qAda.
